
## Triangle inequality

```avatar
image:  9.png
description: |-
  The red diagonal $\vec{V} + \vec{W}$ is always shorter than or equal to the sum of the other two sides ( $\vec{V}\,\text{and}\, \vec{W}$ )

  Mathematically:

  $$||\vec{V} + \vec{W}|| \leq ||\vec{V}|| + ||\vec{W}||$$
```

### Sketch the vectors

1. $3\vec{W}$
2. $\vec{V}+\vec{W}$
3. $2\vec{V}-\vec{W}$

$\vec{V} = (2,4)$
$\vec{W} = (3,-2)$

1. $3\vec{W}$
$$3(3,2) = ((3)\cdot(3) , (3)\cdot(-2))$$
$$3\vec{W} = (9,6)$$
2. $$\vec{V}+\vec{W} = (2,4)+(3,2)$$
$$\vec{V}+\vec{W} = (2+3,4+(-2))$$
$$\vec{V}+\vec{W} = (5,2)$$
3. $$2\vec{V}-\vec{W} = (4,8) - (3,-2)$$
$$2\vec{V} - \vec{W} = (4-3,8-(-2))$$
$$2\vec{V}-\vec{W} = (1,10)$$
