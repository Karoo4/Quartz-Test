### Vector
is a quantity that has both magnitude & direction.

- A vector has a initial and terminal point. 

- A <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">vector</mark> is <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">defined</mark> by its <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">magnitude</mark> and <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">direction</mark> (<mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">regardless</mark>, where its <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">initial point is located.</mark>)

- When a vector has a initial point ( $P$ ) and terminal point ( $Q$ ) , the notation ( $\vec{PQ}$ ) 
	 (This is useful because it <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">indicates the direction</mark> and <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">location of the vector</mark>.)

### Scalar
 A <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">real number</mark> is called a <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">scalar</mark>, in math and physics

### Zero vector
- A vector with initial and terminal point that are the same is called the <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">zero vector</mark>, <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">denoted by 0</mark>
- The (zero vector) is the only vector w/o direction.

### Vector in a plane
- A [[Key Terms#Vector|vector]] in a plane is represented by a directed line segment ( Arrow )
- <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">End points</mark>
	* <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">Initial point</mark> - Starting point of the vector
	* <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">Terminal point</mark>- ending point of the vector 
-  the vector <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">direction</mark> is from the initial to the terminal point.
