

* Two vectors $\vec{U}$ and $\vec{V}$ are colinear , if one is a scalar multiple of the other :
$$(\vec{U} = K\vec{V})$$
* This means they are <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">on the same line (parallel) </mark>, even if they <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">point in opposite directions.</mark>
