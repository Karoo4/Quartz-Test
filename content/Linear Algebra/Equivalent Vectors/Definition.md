- Vectors with the <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">same magnitude and direction</mark> are called (equivalent vectors), we treat equivalent vectors as equal <mark style="background: #FFB8EBA6;">even if they have different initial points</mark> so if  $\vec{v}$ and $\vec{w}$ are equivalent,  we write $\vec{v} = \vec{w}$
